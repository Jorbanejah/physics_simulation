# Special motion

Hello and welcome to a new chapter, today: Special motion

This folder is especially exciting: we finally dive into those unusual motions your mechanics professor mentioned only briefly — the ones that look simple on paper but reveal surprisingly rich dynamics.

Below, you will find the structure of the repository. Explore freely and choose which oscillation you would like to study first.

Two degrees of freedom:

- Double-pendulum: lagrangian derivation of the equations of motion, deterministic chaos and awesome and peculiar animation.
- Spherical pendulum: lagrangian equation of motion and smooth animation. 

One degree of freedom:

- In progress: Nonlinear stiffness, bistability, hysteresis, and the onset of chaos — coming soon.
